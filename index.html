About ProStitch

Limbe's Home of Quality Workwear & Uniforms

We are a team of over 12 professional tailors based in Limbe, Blantyre — specialising in work suits, school uniforms, full uniform sets and custom corporate clothing.

From one tailored suit to a large bulk order, we handle each project with care and precision. We work with schools, hospitals, hotels, security companies, factories and local businesses across southern Malawi.
